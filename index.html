The beta pcs story

We started in a garage. Honestly, we’re still kind of in a garage. Beta PCs exists because computer shops kept either overcharging, over-explaining, or both. So we just build solid PCs, fix broken ones, and try to make you laugh while we’re at it. We’re always tweaking, always improving — which is really just a nice way of saying we’re forever in beta.
